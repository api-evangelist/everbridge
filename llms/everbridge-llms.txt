# Everbridge

> Everbridge provides High Velocity Critical Event Management (CEM), powered by Purpose-built AI, to help organizations know earlier, respond faster, and improve continuously. The platform unifies risk intelligence, communications, and coordinated response across people, assets, operations, and public safety environments to support organizational resilience at enterprise scale.

Last updated: July 29, 2026  
Maintained by: Shaun Bolak, Web Platform Development Manager

**Key differentiators**

- Trusted by over 6,500 customers worldwide.
- Extends beyond traditional IT alerting and incident management to support physical security, business continuity, public safety, and organizational resilience.
- Combines risk intelligence, real-time communications, and coordinated incident response in a unified platform.
- Supports autonomous and human-guided response workflows to improve operational speed, coordination, and continuous resilience improvement.

## Key Products

- [Everbridge 360 High Velocity Critical Event Management](https://www.everbridge.com/platform/critical-event-management/): Critical Event Management platform for anticipating, managing, and responding to crises by combining risk intelligence, communications, and coordinated response across the organization.
- [BC in the Cloud](https://www.everbridge.com/products/bc-in-the-cloud-everbridge/): Business continuity and disaster recovery platform that unifies planning, risk management, and response with automation, real-time insights, and compliance-ready workflows.
- [xMatters](https://www.xmatters.com/): AI-powered incident management and automation platform in the Everbridge suite that integrates monitoring and DevOps tools, orchestrates on-call response, triggers remediation, and supports continuous improvement.
- [Everbridge Travel Protector](https://www.everbridge.com/products/travel-protector/): Travel risk management product that protects traveling and remote employees with real-time risk intelligence, location-based alerts, and two-way communication.
- [Everbridge Control Center](https://www.everbridge.com/products/control-center/): Physical security and control room platform that unifies disparate safety and security systems into a single operating picture with real-time situational awareness and automated response workflows.
- [Everbridge Response Management](https://www.everbridge.com/products/response-management/): Guided incident workflows that connect planning, communications, response execution, and reporting to help teams coordinate consistent, efficient, and accountable action.
- [Everbridge Personal Safety Device](https://www.everbridge.com/products/personal-safety-device/): Wearable personal safety device that provides employees with immediate access to assistance through SOS alerts, fall detection, and real-time location tracking.
- [SnapComms](https://www.snapcomms.com/): Internal communications platform from an Everbridge company that delivers high-visibility, multi-channel messaging to employees across desktop and mobile.
- [RedSky Technologies](https://www.redskytech.com/): Cloud-native E911 and emergency communications platform within the Everbridge suite that helps organizations find callers, route emergency calls, notify internal teams, and support safety compliance.
- [Everbridge Public Warning](https://www.everbridge.com/products/public-warning/): Population alerting and public safety platform that enables governments and authorities to deliver targeted, multi-channel emergency notifications and real-time information to communities.
- [Everbridge Insurance Services](https://www.everbridge.com/products/insurance-services/): Parametric severe-weather insurance services available exclusively to Everbridge 360 customers, designed to align coverage with operational response needs when qualifying conditions are met, subject to policy terms and availability.
