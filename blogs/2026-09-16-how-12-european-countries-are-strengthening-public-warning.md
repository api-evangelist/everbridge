Everbridge Public Warning brings modern and traditional channels together in one platform. Authorities can combine Cell Broadcast, location-based SMS and address-based SMS with television, radio, sirens, email, voice calls, social media, warning apps and digital signage. This multi-channel approach matters because no single method is universal.
