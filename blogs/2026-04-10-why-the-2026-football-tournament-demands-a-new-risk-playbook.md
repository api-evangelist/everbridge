<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Major international sporting tournaments create far more than moments of celebration. They place sustained pressure on infrastructure, security, supply chains, and communications, while exposing organizations to a wide range of operational and reputational risks.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The 2026 football tournament will be no exception. Drawing global attention, it will challenge organizations to operate in a fast-moving environment where disruption can emerge quickly, spread across borders, and escalate without warning. </p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4a343dec">Why this tournament is different&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Major sporting events have always required planning, but 2026 stands apart in both scale and context. Organizations are preparing amid a broader environment of political volatility, evolving security threats, economic uncertainty, and more frequent weather-related disruption.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Matches will be spread across a wide geographic footprint, with the U.S. hosting the majority while Canada and Mexico also play key roles. That footprint creates cross-border dependencies in&nbsp;logistics, workforce movement, and security coordination that will require precision planning.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">At the same time, host city infrastructure will come under sustained pressure. Transport systems will face heavy demand, public infrastructure will be stretched, and the need for services will remain elevated for weeks, not days.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Overlaying all of this is a volatile political and social environment. In the U.S., polarization and policy debates continue to drive protest activity and localized disruption. In Mexico, security conditions and organized crime dynamics create&nbsp;a different set&nbsp;of risks, particularly along transport routes and&nbsp;logistics&nbsp;corridors. Canada faces lower baseline&nbsp;risk, but&nbsp;is still exposed through cross-border supply chains and spillover from U.S.-driven movements.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This is not just a bigger event.&nbsp;It’s&nbsp;a more complex one.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-6f4118ab">Physical security&nbsp;and&nbsp;terrorism&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">One of the most important shifts for organizations is moving away from venue-centric thinking.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Risk during the tournament will extend far beyond stadiums. It will play out across city&nbsp;centers, transport hubs, hotels, fan zones, and any location where large crowds gather.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In the United States, the combination of scale, visibility, and domestic polarization increases exposure to both opportunistic incidents and ideologically motivated violence. Domestic violent extremism (DVE)&nbsp;remains&nbsp;a persistent concern, often decentralized and amplified through online mobilization.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Mexico presents a different profile. Risks are more likely to stem from opportunistic crime and organized criminal activity, including the presence and influence of drug cartels, particularly along key transport and&nbsp;logistics&nbsp;routes. These groups can exert control over certain areas, increasing the likelihood of theft, extortion, and disruption. As a result, employee movement, vendor access, and supply continuity may be affected.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">affected.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="">Canada sits somewhere in between with lower baseline&nbsp;risk but&nbsp;is&nbsp;still exposed to lone-actor threats and the same vulnerabilities associated with crowded public spaces.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Across all three countries,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/terror-alert-system/">terrorism</a>&nbsp;risk has shifted toward low-complexity, high-impact incidents targeting soft locations during high-visibility events.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4e84db33">Protests, politics, and fast-moving disruption&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Major global events increasingly intersect with domestic political dynamics, and 2026 will be no exception.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In the U.S., protest activity is already a regular feature of the operating environment, often tied to elections, policy debates, or global conflicts. These events can scale quickly, shift locations, and disrupt access to key urban areas with little warning.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Canada may experience similar, though typically smaller-scale, demonstrations, often aligned with international or social movements. However, the proximity to U.S. media and digital ecosystems means narratives can spread quickly across borders.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In Mexico, protests may be more localized but can still disrupt city&nbsp;centers, transport routes, and commercial activity, particularly where economic or governance issues are involved.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Across all three countries, mobilization is faster, more digital, and less predictable than in&nbsp;previous&nbsp;event cycles. For organizations, that means disruption is more likely to be sudden and localized but still operationally significant.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-9675a4aa">Cyber threats and disinformation&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">If physical risk is spread across cities,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/cyber-security/">cyber</a>&nbsp;risk is borderless.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Organizations should expect increased phishing activity tied to tickets and travel, as well as attempts to disrupt platforms linked to&nbsp;logistics, hospitality, and communications.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">What has changed is the speed and scale of these threats. AI-enabled tools are making attacks more convincing and easier to deploy at volume, while disinformation can spread rapidly,&nbsp;particularly when tied to political narratives or public safety messaging.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The cross-border nature of the tournament adds another layer of risk. A disruption affecting systems in one country can have immediate consequences in another, particularly where operations rely on shared platforms or infrastructure.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-cc6ee67f">Supply chains, infrastructure, and extreme weather&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">For many organizations, the most immediate impact of the tournament will be operational rather than&nbsp;security-driven.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Host cities will experience sustained congestion, restricted routes, and pressure on infrastructure. In the U.S., this will be most visible in large metropolitan areas where systems are already&nbsp;operating&nbsp;near capacity. In Mexico, disruption may be more uneven, particularly along&nbsp;logistics&nbsp;corridors with existing infrastructure constraints. In Canada, capacity limitations and cross-border flow may present the greatest challenge.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">At the same time,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/inclement-weather-solutions/">extreme weather</a>&nbsp;is becoming a constant factor. Heatwaves, storms, and flooding can disrupt transport, strain energy systems, and reduce recovery time between incidents.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-ed52e608">Workforce disruption and duty of care&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Employees will be navigating congested cities, altered transport routes, and, in some cases, heightened security environments. In the U.S., this may also include exposure to harassment or intimidation influenced by online misinformation.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In Mexico, duty of care considerations may focus more on safe travel routes and local security conditions. In Canada, the primary challenge may be disruption and communication,&nbsp;ensuring employees can move safely and stay informed.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Across all three countries, organizations face the same core question: can employees get where they need to be, and&nbsp;operate&nbsp;safely once they arrive?&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-41c4f816">Managing complexity: When information becomes the challenge&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Events at this scale generate a constant flow of information but not always clarity.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Organizations will be&nbsp;operating&nbsp;alongside multiple stakeholders, from local authorities to national agencies and private partners. The challenge is not access to data, but the ability to filter,&nbsp;validate, and act on it quickly.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Those that perform best are not the ones with the most information, but the ones that can turn insight into action without delay.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-2a453bd4">What organizations should do now&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Organizations that navigate events like this successfully tend to follow a consistent approach.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">They start planning early, but avoid rigid frameworks, building flexible response models that can adapt as conditions change. They&nbsp;establish&nbsp;clear command structures and communication protocols in advance, reducing confusion during incidents. They invest in systems that enable fast, targeted communication across multiple channels.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">They also build relationships ahead of time with local partners, authorities, and internal&nbsp;stakeholders&nbsp;so coordination is already in place when needed.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">And importantly, they test their plans. Exercises, walkthroughs, and scenario-based simulations help&nbsp;identify&nbsp;gaps before they become real-world failures.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-6c2404b7">Final thoughts&nbsp;for risk leaders&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The&nbsp;2026&nbsp;football&nbsp;tournament&nbsp;will be more than a sporting event. It will be a sustained test of how well organizations can&nbsp;operate&nbsp;in a complex, fast-moving risk environment.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The organizations that succeed&nbsp;won’t&nbsp;be those reacting in real time.&nbsp;They’ll&nbsp;be the ones that prepare early, stay flexible, and&nbsp;maintain&nbsp;visibility across their operations,&nbsp;across cities, and across borders.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>
