</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">

<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-d51277ef">Understanding the risk formula</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/risk-intelligence/">Risk intelligence</a>&nbsp;isn&#8217;t&nbsp;just about&nbsp;identifying&nbsp;threats.&nbsp;It&#8217;s&nbsp;a systematic formula that organizations can follow to improve resilience across all types of critical events, whether geopolitical, weather-related, cyber, or physical security incidents.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The formula is straightforward: a threat creates risk or exposes a vulnerability, which leads to consequences. Once your organization&nbsp;identifies&nbsp;unacceptable consequences, you can begin to&nbsp;identify&nbsp;gaps in your defenses and develop&nbsp;appropriate countermeasures&nbsp;and&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/risk-mitigation/">mitigation strategies</a>.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This framework applies universally, but geopolitical risks present unique challenges that make them particularly difficult to manage.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-6a2d02c8">The&nbsp;unique challenge of geopolitical risk</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Geopolitical risk encompasses conflicts, policy decisions, election results, and various forms of political instability. These events can manifest as&nbsp;different types&nbsp;of threats:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Kinetic threats occur when regional instability escalates into actual military conflict.&nbsp;</li>



<li>Cyber threats&nbsp;emerge&nbsp;from nation-state actors and activist groups triggered by specific situations or decisions.&nbsp;</li>



<li>Infrastructure impacts can include energy crises and other disruptions to business operations that may seem unrelated to the original event.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">What makes geopolitical risk particularly challenging is its cascading nature. Unlike weather events where you can predict power outages and road closures, or&nbsp;cyber attacks&nbsp;where you can&nbsp;anticipate&nbsp;certain impacts based on the attack type, geopolitical events create dynamic situations where one decision or comment can trigger risks from multiple angles.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Some impacts&nbsp;aren&#8217;t&nbsp;immediate. While other risks present themselves right away, geopolitical vulnerabilities might not surface for weeks or even years.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-14e9b407">Real-world examples of cascading geopolitical risks</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-80271cae">The&nbsp;Russia-Ukraine&nbsp;conflict</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This conflict between a nuclear power and a former republic has created far-reaching consequences:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Direct kinetic threats in Eastern Europe&nbsp;</li>



<li>Global food security issues, as both countries are among the world&#8217;s largest wheat producers&nbsp;</li>



<li>Supply chain disruptions affecting African nations, leading to hunger crises and regional instability&nbsp;</li>



<li>Energy supply complications for NATO countries previously dependent on Russian energy&nbsp;</li>



<li>Reduced redundancies increasing the likelihood of energy crises in Europe&nbsp;</li>



<li>Uncertainty over security assurances as diplomatic relationships evolve&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-de4225ed">U.S.&nbsp;political decisions</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">American policy and decision-making have massive global effects, regardless of political perspective:&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Government restructuring affecting international presence&nbsp;</li>



<li>Potential cuts to forecasting agencies&nbsp;impacting&nbsp;predictive weather analysis&nbsp;</li>



<li>Power vacuums in developing regions that countries like China and Russia can exploit&nbsp;</li>



<li>Political extremism stemming from domestic divisions&nbsp;</li>



<li>Financial implications from trade policies, tariffs, and market regulatio</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Each of these situations&nbsp;demonstrates&nbsp;how a singular event or decision can create risks that seem myopic to one region but have cascading impacts for weeks and years to come.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-66d81c46">Building&nbsp;resilience through strategic mitigation</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The goal&nbsp;isn&#8217;t&nbsp;risk avoidance, which is impossible. Instead, organizations should focus on identifying unacceptable consequences to their assets and building mitigation strategies to&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/organizational-resilience-solutions/">improve resilience</a>.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4260eacc">The&nbsp;three modification strategies&nbsp;</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Organizations&nbsp;can mitigate risk by&nbsp;modifying&nbsp;one of three elements:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>The&nbsp;asset:</strong>&nbsp;If&nbsp;you&nbsp;can&#8217;t&nbsp;control the threat or vulnerabilities&nbsp;immediately,&nbsp;modify&nbsp;your asset.&nbsp;Think&nbsp;installing locks and security systems in a dangerous neighborhood, carrying pepper spray, changing transit times, or even&nbsp;relocating.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>The&nbsp;threat:</strong>&nbsp;In&nbsp;some cases, you can take actions that reduce the threat itself, though this is often the most difficult to control.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>The&nbsp;vulnerability:</strong>&nbsp;Build redundancies and alternative approaches. For example, if&nbsp;you&#8217;re&nbsp;concerned about shipping lanes in the Middle East,&nbsp;modify&nbsp;your routes or create backup supply chains.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">By&nbsp;modifying&nbsp;at least one of these elements,&nbsp;organizations&nbsp;can take concrete steps to build resilience, even when you&nbsp;can&#8217;t&nbsp;control all aspects of the situation.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-82b1f046">The&nbsp;power of proactive intelligence</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The best way to mitigate geopolitical threats is through forward-leaning, proactive intelligence. This means looking around the corner to&nbsp;identify&nbsp;potential threats and cascading impacts by analyzing&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/geographic-trends-shaping-the-global-security-landscape/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">historical trends</a>.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-591ddeac">Key questions for threat assessment</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Organizations should ask themselves:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Does your organization or sector face&nbsp;targeting&nbsp;from&nbsp;cyber criminal&nbsp;groups?&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>



<li>Has your industry been targeted by domestic&nbsp;violent extremism?&nbsp;</li>



<li>Do you have close ties to organizations that might become targets during geopolitical tensions?&nbsp;</li>



<li>Does your leadership have high-profile political leanings that could attract attention?&nbsp;</li>



<li>Are you relying on supply chains in volatile zones?&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Good intelligence puts you in a better position to detect threats and deter them from&nbsp;impacting&nbsp;your organization. When you&nbsp;can&#8217;t&nbsp;avoid risk completely, intelligence helps you understand how to mitigate, transform, and create after-action reviews that make you more resilient going forward.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-c0e11f2d">Leveraging&nbsp;AI for&nbsp;enhanced intelligence</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><a href="https://www.everbridge.com/platform/purpose-built-ai/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Artificial intelligence</a>&nbsp;can significantly enhance your risk monitoring capabilities:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Identifying&nbsp;misinformation in an environment filled with disinformation&nbsp;</li>



<li>Adapting and adjusting cyber networks and supply chains&nbsp;</li>



<li>Building redundancies in supply chain communication and automation&nbsp;</li>



<li>Supporting every step of the intelligence cycle, from collecting information to analyzing it and&nbsp;disseminating&nbsp;insights to your people, assets, and customers&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The combination of good intelligence, effective technology, and solid processes creates a powerful framework for mitigating geopolitical risks that might otherwise seem difficult to&nbsp;identify&nbsp;and address.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-1b5e8a80">Protecting&nbsp;traveling populations</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">For organizations with employees who travel internationally, geopolitical instability creates immediate and personal risks. Business travelers often find themselves navigating unfamiliar environments where the psychological toll can be just as challenging as physical risks.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">When we talk about resilience,&nbsp;we&#8217;re&nbsp;not only referring to infrastructure or organizational systems.&nbsp;We&#8217;re&nbsp;talking about human resilience. This is no longer just a duty of care issue.&nbsp;It&#8217;s&nbsp;about employee performance, productivity, and trust in the organization.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">When employees feel safe and know their well-being is prioritized,&nbsp;they&#8217;re&nbsp;more engaged, more confident, and more willing to accept international assignments. This makes your organization more agile and competitive.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-f7e5358b">The&nbsp;three-phase framework for traveler protection</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-69c15d22">Phase 1: Pre-trip preparation&nbsp;</h4>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The most effective way to protect travelers in unstable regions is to support them before they even book their trip. The&nbsp;<a href="https://www.iso.org/obp/ui/en/#iso:std:iso:31030:ed-1:v1:en" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">ISO 31030</a>&nbsp;standard emphasizes that pre-travel planning is at the core of&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/travel-risk-management/">travel risk management</a>.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Comprehensive&nbsp;risk assessments:</strong>&nbsp;No two travelers and no two trips are alike. A senior engineer working at a remote mine faces&nbsp;very different&nbsp;risks than a junior employee attending a training session. A first-time traveler needs more guidance than someone who has traveled to the region for years.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Tailored&nbsp;briefings:</strong>&nbsp;Generic advisories&nbsp;aren&#8217;t&nbsp;enough. Effective pre-trip briefings should cover:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Cultural norms and etiquette&nbsp;</li>



<li>Local laws and regulations&nbsp;</li>



<li>The wider political and regional landscape&nbsp;</li>



<li>Risks of unrest tied to elections or other political events&nbsp;</li>



<li>Infrastructure vulnerabilities&nbsp;</li>



<li>Natural hazards&nbsp;</li>



<li>Cyber crime&nbsp;threats&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Contingency&nbsp;planning:</strong>&nbsp;Evacuation and medical emergency response plans are particularly important in regions where instability can escalate quickly. The cost of being prepared is always lower than the cost of responding unprepared during a crisis.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Local&nbsp;expertise:</strong>&nbsp;Leverage teams with on-the-ground knowledge. In-country teams familiar with the culture, geography, and detailed dynamics of the threat landscape can provide real-time insights and immediate response capabilities that are difficult to replicate with remote monitoring alone.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Traveler&nbsp;empowerment</strong>: Equipping travelers means more than providing&nbsp;an assistance&nbsp;phone number. Provide:</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Extensive travel advisories&nbsp;</li>



<li>Guidance on cyber hygiene&nbsp;</li>



<li>Cultural orientation materials&nbsp;</li>



<li>Mobile tools accessible at any time&nbsp;</li>



<li>Scenario planning that walks travelers through potential disruptions like sudden demonstrations, airport closures, or communication breakdowns&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Travelers who understand not just the&nbsp;risks,&nbsp;but also practical response steps feel more informed, resilient, and supported.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-e141a014">Phase 2: In-country support</h4>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Once travelers are in-country, ongoing visibility, connection, and support become critical. Geopolitical risk is dynamic and can change extremely quickly. Protests can erupt, infrastructure can fail, and health risks can escalate unexpectedly.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Real-time monitoring:</strong>&nbsp;Organizations need&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/products/travel-protector/">tools</a>&nbsp;that track both the threat environment and employee locations so they can quickly adapt when conditions change.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Maintained&nbsp;connectivity:</strong>&nbsp;Mobile infrastructure can be&nbsp;impacted&nbsp;during major incidents, but employees need access to 24/7 support. Ensure redundant communication methods so that if one channel fails, alternatives exist.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Crisis&nbsp;response execution:</strong>&nbsp;This is where proactive planning meets practical execution. If evacuation plans or in-country security teams are in place, response is more immediate. Travelers can be guided via safe routes, advised whether to move or shelter in place, or taken to medical facilities if needed.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">If these services&nbsp;aren&#8217;t&nbsp;retained&nbsp;while a traveler is in-country, you need to be prepared to mobilize local support quickly. This requires clear escalation protocols and defined lines of&nbsp;authority&nbsp;so internal decisions can be made without hesitation or delay.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Tabletop&nbsp;exercises:</strong>&nbsp;Run rehearsals for crisis response to ensure everyone knows their role. These exercises emphasize the importance of communication, both internal and external, ensuring that employees and potentially their loved ones are kept informed during critical incidents.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In moments of crisis, particularly during volatile geopolitical instability, reassurance is&nbsp;almost as&nbsp;important as action.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-47169caf">Phase 3: Post-trip review&nbsp;</h4>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The journey&nbsp;doesn&#8217;t&nbsp;end when the plane touches down at home. True resilience means learning from every trip.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Traveler&nbsp;debriefing:</strong>&nbsp;Post-trip reviews provide opportunities to:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Debrief travelers on their experiences&nbsp;</li>



<li>Capture lessons learned&nbsp;</li>



<li>Support mental health if the journey was stressful or traumatic&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Organizational&nbsp;benefits:</strong>&nbsp;When post-trip insights are fed back into your internal risk intelligence system, they strengthen your overall strategic framework. Firsthand experiences&nbsp;validate&nbsp;or challenge assumptions in your risk modeling, helping you make future decisions more accurately.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Continuous&nbsp;improvement:</strong>&nbsp;Integrate real-world lessons into pre-travel guidance for the next trip and the next traveler. This ensures that your travelers are better prepared for the realities of potentially unstable environments.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Building&nbsp;trust:</strong>&nbsp;When employees feel their voices are heard, they gain confidence in their organization.&nbsp;They&#8217;re&nbsp;more willing to travel again, more engaged, and more resilient in the face of future challenges.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This creates a continuous improvement loop where resilience grows with every journey.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-8157946d">Making&nbsp;resilience a living practice</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The principles of preparation, proactive intelligence monitoring, and resilience building&nbsp;shouldn&#8217;t&nbsp;just live on a shelf in a policy handbook. They should be living principles that are iteratively reviewed and practiced internally.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-f89dd79b">Improving employee training</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The most effective training&nbsp;isn&#8217;t&nbsp;a one-off event.&nbsp;It&#8217;s&nbsp;a continuous process that creates ongoing awareness over time.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Scenario-based training:</strong>&nbsp;Spend time walking employees through realistic disruptions they might face at their destination, such as protests, airport closures, or cyber risks. Talk through how to respond, what actions to take, and what steps are available.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Feedback&nbsp;loops:</strong>&nbsp;Use real-life lessons learned from colleagues who have been through challenging experiences. Refine future sessions by building real-world scenarios into training.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><strong>Consistent&nbsp;rhythm:</strong>&nbsp;Training&nbsp;should be an always-on&nbsp;practice, not a&nbsp;sporadic&nbsp;initiative. Organizations should consider:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>General traveler safety training on a quarterly or biannual basis&nbsp;</li>



<li>Destination-specific training before any employee is deployed to higher-risk regions&nbsp;</li>



<li>Ensuring the cadence is&nbsp;appropriate to&nbsp;the risk level&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">

<p class="">This&nbsp;makes both travelers and the organization more resilient.</p>
