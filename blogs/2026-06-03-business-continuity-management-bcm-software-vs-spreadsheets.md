This article compares BC in the Cloud, Everbridge's business continuity management platform, with traditional spreadsheet-based approaches for organizations managing continuity programs. As programs scale, Excel-based methods prove insufficient compared to dedicated BCM software offering standardized templates, automated workflows, centralized reporting, and audit-ready documentation. Everbridge presents its platform as the solution for organizations that have outgrown spreadsheets and need enterprise-grade resilience tooling.
