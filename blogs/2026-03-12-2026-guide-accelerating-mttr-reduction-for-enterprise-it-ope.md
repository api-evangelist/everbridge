</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Mean&nbsp;time to repair&nbsp;(MTTR) measures the average time&nbsp;required&nbsp;to resolve an incident after it has been detected. It is one of the most important operational metrics for evaluating the effectiveness of IT incident management.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">A lower MTTR indicates that teams can quickly diagnose and resolve incidents before they significantly affect services or users.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">For large enterprises, MTTR directly influences:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Business continuity&nbsp;</li>



<li>Regulatory compliance&nbsp;</li>



<li>Customer experience&nbsp;</li>



<li>Operational costs&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Downtime is increasingly expensive. Industry research estimates that the growing complexity of AI-driven infrastructure has increased incident volumes dramatically, costing large enterprises millions annually in operational disruption.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
