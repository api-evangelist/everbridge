Everbridge's onboarding approach guides new Critical Event Management platform customers through a structured 90-day implementation journey broken into four phases: day one kickoff, day 30 discovery, day 60 hands-on configuration, and day 90 launch. The program combines early platform access, hands-on learning, and expert consultation to help organizations establish a strong foundation for future expansion. By launch day, customers gain configured systems, trained administrators, and resources for ongoing growth.
