<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-75315d31">The&nbsp;new&nbsp;age of AI-driven&nbsp;cyber&nbsp;threats&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">A powerful force is redefining the 2026 security landscape: the weaponization of artificial intelligence. What began as attackers experimenting with generative AI has evolved into a core part of their modus&nbsp;operandi, fundamentally altering the nature of cyber risk.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">AI is no longer a novelty: it is the primary driver of change, accelerating the speed, scale, and sophistication of digital threats. To build resilient defenses, organizations must pivot from traditional security measures to a strategic approach designed for this AI-driven environment.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In this post, we explore the key transformations ahead, from hyper-personalized phishing to fully autonomous ransomware. For a comprehensive analysis and detailed protection strategies, read the full <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/2026-outlook/">Everbridge 2026 Regional risk &amp; resilience outlook</a>.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-6a3d30b4">The&nbsp;rise of AI-powered&nbsp;deception&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In 2025, we&nbsp;witnessed&nbsp;a sharp increase in AI-generated phishing and&nbsp;deepfake-enabled social engineering. Threat actors now routinely use large language models (LLMs) to craft convincing, tailored phishing content at a massive scale. This development dramatically lowers the barrier to entry for running sophisticated campaigns that were once the domain of highly skilled, well-resourced groups.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Deepfake audio and video are also increasingly used to impersonate executives in high-value business email compromise (BEC)&nbsp;scams. These AI-supported campaigns manipulate employees, customers, and partners with a level of credibility that is difficult for humans and traditional&nbsp;security measures&nbsp;to detect. The content is more targeted, the language&nbsp;more&nbsp;natural, and the overall execution more believable, making them significantly more dangerous.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-2e774a8f">From&nbsp;human-operated to&nbsp;autonomous&nbsp;attacks&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">2025 also marked an inflection point for automated offensive operations. Security research and public reporting highlighted the first large-scale, AI-orchestrated attacks. In these campaigns, threat actors used&nbsp;<a href="https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/ai-jailbreak" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">jailbroken</a>&nbsp;or custom-trained models to automate most of the intrusion lifecycle,&nbsp;including&nbsp;reconnaissance, exploitation, credential theft, lateral movement, and data exfiltration, often executed across many targets at once.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">While debate continues about the true autonomy of these early campaigns, the key takeaway for 2026 planning is undeniable: AI is no longer just&nbsp;assisting&nbsp;human operators. It is being embedded as the execution engine within adversary tooling and frameworks. This&nbsp;represents&nbsp;a fundamental shift from human-in-the-loop attacks to machine-led operations.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Looking ahead, we expect this trend to mature into continuous, AI-driven attack operations. Threat actors will&nbsp;likely deploy&nbsp;AI agents that&nbsp;operate&nbsp;around the clock, constantly probing external attack surfaces, chaining exploits, and adapting in real time to defender responses. These agents can spread across&nbsp;numerous&nbsp;targets simultaneously, automatically generating phishing lures, infrastructure, and malware variants at a speed that manual operators cannot match. As a result, fully or&nbsp;nearly fully&nbsp;automated attack chains will become common, and the background level of malicious activity on enterprise networks will continue its upward climb.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-97afdb37">Fighting&nbsp;automation with&nbsp;automation&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">As attackers&nbsp;leverage&nbsp;AI, security teams must respond in kind. The clear path forward is to fight automation with automation. In 2026, we expect to see much wider adoption of AI-based security validation. This includes always-on penetration testing, continuous vulnerability assessments, and autonomous attack surface management running across enterprise infrastructure.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Instead of relying on annual audits or occasional <a href="https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/red_team_exercise" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">red team exercises,</a> AI agents will continuously scan networks, applications, identities, and configurations. They will flag gaps and, where appropriate, trigger automated remediation before malicious actors can exploit them. This approach shortens the time from vulnerability discovery to fix from weeks or months down to hours or days, transforming point-in-time assessments into a continuous, closed-loop process of <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/5-focus-areas-for-cyber-resilience/">cyber resilience</a>. </p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This dynamic, where both attackers and defenders are scaling through automation, has critical implications. Automated attacks will demand an automated response. Security operations&nbsp;centers&nbsp;(SOCs) that rely purely on manual processes will be unable to keep pace with AI-accelerated campaigns. Organizations will need tightly integrated detection and response pipelines that combine AI-based analytics, security orchestration, and pre-approved remediation actions to&nbsp;contain&nbsp;threats at machine speed.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-d1c883cb">Ransomware 5.0: The&nbsp;autonomous&nbsp;threat&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Ransomware is a clear example of where this shift will be most visible. We are already seeing the early signs of AI-assisted ransomware, along with proof-of-concept autonomous frameworks that use LLMs to run campaigns end-to-end. The next evolution,&nbsp;referred to as&nbsp;Ransomware 5.0, marks the transformation of ransomware from a human-operated campaign into a semi- or&nbsp;fully-autonomous&nbsp;operation.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In this new model, AI is embedded into every stage of the attack:&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Initial&nbsp;access:</strong>&nbsp;AI generates hyper-personalized, multilingual phishing lures, voice clones for vishing, and deepfakes to impersonate trusted figures, maximizing the&nbsp;breach&nbsp;success rate.&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Reconnaissance:</strong>&nbsp;Once inside, AI agents dynamically map the network,&nbsp;identify&nbsp;high-value data stores,&nbsp;locate&nbsp;shadow IT, and pinpoint critical misconfigurations in minutes.&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Lateral&nbsp;movement:</strong>&nbsp;The AI platform chains together multiple exploits, generates bespoke exploit code on the fly, and moves across systems at speeds impossible for human operators.&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Payload&nbsp;evasion:</strong>&nbsp;The AI continuously refactors the&nbsp;malware&#8217;s&nbsp;code and behavior in real-time based on the defensive tools it detects, making signature-based detection ineffective.&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Extortion and&nbsp;negotiation:</strong>&nbsp;Intelligent extortion bots analyze exfiltrated data to calculate the&nbsp;optimal&nbsp;ransom amount. The system can even manage negotiations using sophisticated, convincing language.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">This shift to autonomous operations dramatically reduces&nbsp;“dwell time,”&nbsp;the period an attacker&nbsp;remains&nbsp;undetected,&nbsp;from days or weeks down to mere hours. It also&nbsp;dissolves the line between basic commodity malware and sophisticated nation-state-level attacks, allowing smaller criminal groups to execute complex campaigns.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-4bde5ba4">Preparing for the AI-driven&nbsp;threat&nbsp;landscape&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">To cope with this challenging environment, organizations must combine strong architectural controls with AI-driven&nbsp;defenses&nbsp;of their own.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-5f1e49e5">Adopt a&nbsp;zero&nbsp;trust&nbsp;architecture&nbsp;</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class=""><a href="https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/zero-trust-architecture/implementing-zta/demystifying-zero-trust" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Zero&nbsp;trust</a>&nbsp;principles will be central to mitigating the impact of AI-driven campaigns. Built on a foundation of explicit verification, least-privilege access, and the assumption of breach, this model helps limit the blast radius when an attacker gains access.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In practice, organizations should reduce their&nbsp;infrastructure&#8217;s&nbsp;exposure to the public internet. Moving away from traditional, perimeter-focused security toward&nbsp;private access&nbsp;models that rely on strong identity, device trust, and context-aware controls is essential. Every user, device, and service must be evaluated constantly based on posture and&nbsp;behavior, not just its network location.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-f39ce2d3">Leverage AI-powered&nbsp;defenses&nbsp;</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">To counter AI-generated threats, organizations must deploy their own AI-based&nbsp;defenses.&nbsp;Behavioral&nbsp;analytics for users, entities, and workloads will become critical for spotting subtle anomalies that static signatures and legacy rule sets miss. These systems can detect the fast-changing, polymorphic malware variants generated by adversarial AI.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Furthermore, <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/the-future-of-security-ai/">integrating AI</a> into security operations will be non-negotiable. This means using AI to analyze threat intelligence, prioritize alerts, and orchestrate automated response actions. By doing so, security teams can scale their efforts and respond to threats at the machine speed required to counter autonomous attacks. </p>
</div>
</div>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-b77818d6">Embrace&nbsp;continuous&nbsp;security&nbsp;validation&nbsp;</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The era of periodic security testing is over. The dynamic nature of AI-driven threats requires continuous validation of security controls. Automated penetration testing and attack surface management tools can constantly&nbsp;probe for&nbsp;weaknesses,&nbsp;providing&nbsp;a real-time view of an organization&#8217;s security posture. This allows teams to proactively&nbsp;identify&nbsp;and remediate vulnerabilities before they can be exploited by an automated attacker.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The weaponization of AI&nbsp;represents&nbsp;a pivotal moment in cybersecurity. Attackers are&nbsp;leveraging&nbsp;automation to create more sophisticated, scalable, and evasive threats than ever before. To remain resilient, organizations must embrace a new defensive paradigm,&nbsp;one that is proactive, automated, and architecturally sound. By adopting&nbsp;zero&nbsp;trust,&nbsp;leveraging&nbsp;AI-driven defenses, and committing to continuous validation, businesses can prepare for the challenges of 2026 and beyond.&nbsp;</p>
