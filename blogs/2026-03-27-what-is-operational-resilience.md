<p class=""><a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/operational-resilience/">Operational resilience</a> is defined by regulators such as the <a href="https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/operational-resilience" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">UK FCA</a> as <em>the ability of an organization to prevent, adapt, respond to, recover, and learn from operational disruptions while continuing to deliver important business services within acceptable impact tolerances.</em> </p>



<p class="">This definition emphasizes not just recovery, but sustained service delivery during disruption. In practice, operational resilience goes beyond traditional risk management by focusing on end-to-end services, their dependencies, and measurable customer outcomes. For resilient organizations, this means identifying what truly matters (critical services), understanding vulnerabilities, and ensuring continuity under stress—whether from cyber incidents, third-party failures, or systemic shocks. </p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-1762c6c1">Operational resilience vs business continuity</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column mb-2 is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Operational resilience and business continuity are closely related but serve distinct roles in managing disruption. While <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/business-continuity/" id="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/business-continuity/" type="link">business continuity</a> focuses on restoring operations after an incident, operational resilience takes a broader, proactive approach—ensuring critical services continue within defined impact tolerances even during disruption. The comparison below outlines the key differences to help organizations determine when and how to apply each.</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns evbg-table is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<figure class="wp-block-table is-style-stripes"><table class="has-000000-color has-text-color has-link-color has-fixed-layout"><thead><tr><th><strong>Category</strong></th><th><strong>Operational&nbsp;resilience</strong></th><th><strong>Business&nbsp;continuity</strong>&nbsp;</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Scope&nbsp;</td><td>End-to-end service delivery (important business services)&nbsp;</td><td>Processes, systems, and recovery plans&nbsp;</td></tr><tr><td>Trigger&nbsp;</td><td>Proactive and continuous&nbsp;</td><td>Reactive (post-disruption)&nbsp;</td></tr><tr><td>Primary&nbsp;metrics&nbsp;</td><td><br />Impact tolerance&nbsp;</td><td>RTO (Recovery Time Objective), RPO (Recovery Point Objective)&nbsp;</td></tr><tr><td>Accountable&nbsp;owners&nbsp;</td><td>Executive leadership / board-level oversight&nbsp;</td><td>IT / risk / operations teams&nbsp;</td></tr><tr><td>Core&nbsp;outputs&nbsp;</td><td>IBS mapping, dependency mapping, scenario testing, tolerances&nbsp;</td><td>BCP plans, disaster recovery procedures&nbsp;</td></tr></tbody></table></figure>
</div>

<div class="wp-block-columns mt-2 is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">

<p class="">Takeaway:&nbsp;Use operational resilience to proactively ensure critical services&nbsp;remain&nbsp;within acceptable impact thresholds, and business continuity to restore operations after disruption.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a3b72d15">Why operational resilience matters</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Modern organizations operate in increasingly complex, interconnected ecosystems. Disruptions can cascade quickly. Operational resilience ensures organizations maintain trust, meet regulatory expectations, and protect customers even under stress. </p>



<p class="">Disruption is an unavoidable reality. With increasingly sophisticated cyber threats, geopolitical uncertainties, supply chain failures, natural disasters, and a hyperconnected digital world, the question is no longer <em>if</em> a critical incident will occur, but <em>when.</em> The ability to anticipate and mitigate such incidents can mean the difference between navigating the storm successfully or facing significant losses.  </p>



<p class="">Beyond financial impacts, failing to maintain operations during a crisis can severely harm your reputation and strain relationships with customers and vendors. In some industries, it could even lead to regulatory penalties. Being prepared is not just an advantage, it’s a necessity. </p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-27a279bf">How to build operational resilience</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Building operational resilience requires a structured, outcomes-driven approach that connects strategy with execution. Organizations must&nbsp;identify&nbsp;critical services, define acceptable levels of disruption, and continuously test their ability to stay within those thresholds.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Identify&nbsp;Important Business Services (IBS)</strong>&nbsp;and produce an IBS register&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Define impact tolerances</strong>&nbsp;for each IBS and document measurable thresholds&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Map dependencies</strong>&nbsp;across people, processes, technology, and third parties to create a dependency map&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Assess third-party criticality</strong>&nbsp;and produce a third-party criticality matrix&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Conduct <a href="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/conducting-effective-tabletop-exercises-for-emergency-preparedness/" id="https://www.everbridge.com/blog/conducting-effective-tabletop-exercises-for-emergency-preparedness/" type="link">scenario testing</a></strong> and document a scenario-test plan </li>



<li><strong>Develop response strategies</strong>&nbsp;and create communications playbooks&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Embed monitoring and reporting</strong>&nbsp;with defined KPIs and dashboards&nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>Establish continuous improvement cycles</strong>&nbsp;with a formal review cadence&nbsp;</li>
</ol>
</div>
</div>
