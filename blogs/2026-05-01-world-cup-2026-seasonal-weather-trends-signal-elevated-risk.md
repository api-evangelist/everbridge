<p class="">As anticipation builds for the 2026 FIFA World Cup across the United States, Mexico, and Canada, a growing concern is taking&nbsp;center&nbsp;stage alongside the action on the pitch: extreme heat and its cascading impact on safety and operations.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Heat is no longer a background risk; it is a primary operational challenge. Elevated temperatures, especially when combined with high humidity, can significantly increase “feels-like” conditions and create dangerous environments for players, staff, and spectators alike.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Seasonal signals reinforce this concern throughout North America, with above-normal temperatures forecast during the tournament window. This increases the likelihood of longer-duration and more widespread heat&nbsp;events.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Recent&nbsp;<a href="https://www.msn.com/en-in/sports/fifa_world_cup/this-year-s-world-cup-games-could-be-sizzling/ar-AA2200e4" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">external coverage</a>&nbsp;has also highlighted the growing concern that World Cup matches could be played in extremely hot conditions, reinforcing that this is not just a meteorological signal, but a widely recognized operational risk.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-b71e071e">Why extreme heat is a primary operational risk for World Cup 2026&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Extreme heat is not just a weather condition. It is a direct driver of operational disruption.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Elevated temperatures increase the risk of heat-related illness, strain medical services, complicate crowd management, and disrupt event operations. In major metropolitan areas such as New York/New Jersey, Dallas, Mexico City, Toronto, and Los Angeles, these risks are amplified by urban heat island effects, increased energy demand, and pressure on critical infrastructure.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">For&nbsp;<a href="https://www.everbridge.com/use-cases/event-risk-management-solutions/" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">large-scale events</a>&nbsp;like the World Cup, the combination of&nbsp;high temperatures, dense crowds, and prolonged outdoor exposure creates conditions where small disruptions can escalate quickly.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-51b6c963">Where heat risk is expected to be most pronounced&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The strongest heat signals are concentrated across the western and central United States, where persistent dryness is likely to intensify both the duration and severity of heat events.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In southern-tier cities such as Atlanta, Dallas, Houston, and Miami, typical June and July&nbsp;high temperatures&nbsp;already range from the middle 80s to upper 90s °F (30 to 35°C). When combined with Gulf moisture, heat index values can&nbsp;frequently&nbsp;exceed 105 to 110 °F (40 to 43°C), creating dangerous conditions during peak afternoon match windows.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Across southern Canada, including major population&nbsp;centers&nbsp;like Toronto, average summer highs in the 70s to 80s °F (21 to 30°C) can trend warmer through July. While less persistent than in the southern United States, these episodic heat events can still pose meaningful risks, particularly for large outdoor crowds that may be less acclimatized.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In Mexico, the most intense and consistent heat risk is focused across northern and central regions, including Monterrey and Mexico City. Monterrey&nbsp;frequently&nbsp;experiences daytime highs approaching 100°F (38°C), with higher&nbsp;apparent&nbsp;temperatures under humid conditions. Mexico City, despite its elevation, can see temperatures rise into the mid to upper 70s °F (22 to 27°C), where strong solar radiation and thinner air can accelerate heat stress.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The operational challenge is not just peak temperature. It is the combination of intensity, duration, and prolonged exposure across large populations.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a34cf7ca">Heat rarely occurs in isolation&nbsp;</h3>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">These atmospheric conditions are unlikely to occur in isolation. Seasonal trends point toward compound risk scenarios, where multiple hazards unfold in close succession or at the same time.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">While much of the focus is on extreme heat, broader June and July climate patterns suggest&nbsp;additional&nbsp;challenges that will need to be managed in parallel.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">An&nbsp;increase&nbsp;in thunderstorm activity across eastern and southern North America may bring lightning, heavy rainfall, and localized disruption. In western regions, wildfire risk and associated smoke may degrade air quality and visibility. The early months of the Atlantic and eastern Pacific hurricane seasons also&nbsp;coincide&nbsp;with the tournament.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">These&nbsp;overlapping&nbsp;hazards&nbsp;can amplify one another. Heat combined with poor air quality, or storms disrupting already strained transport systems, can quickly increase operational complexity.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-8edf82f3">A distributed event with localized risk profiles&nbsp;</h2>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">Unlike a single-site event, the World Cup is a distributed, multi-city operation spanning 16 host locations across three countries, each with distinct climate profiles and seasonal risk factors.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">The structure of the tournament amplifies this complexity. Matches are often scheduled during peak afternoon and early evening heat. Large crowds&nbsp;remain&nbsp;exposed to outdoor conditions for extended periods. Operations depend heavily on transportation networks, energy systems, and public safety infrastructure.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="">In this environment, weather becomes more than a planning consideration. It becomes a driver of real-time operational decision making.&nbsp;</p>
</div>
</div>



<div class="wp-block-columns is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-9d6595d7 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
