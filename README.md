Everbridge is a global critical event management (CEM) software company whose platform helps organizations anticipate, manage and respond to disruption — unifying risk intelligence, mass communication and coordinated response across people, assets, operations and public safety environments. The Everbridge 360 platform serves over 6,500 customers across business services, energy and utilities, federal and local government, healthcare, financial services, higher education, manufacturing and transportation. Its API estate is published on a public ReadMe developer hub as thirteen OpenAPI documents covering roughly 473 operations: the EB Suite REST API (contacts, groups, users, incidents, notifications, scheduling, calendars, locations, conference bridges, custom roles), a Communications API with native OASIS Common Alerting Protocol and FEMA IPAWS delivery, a GraphQL CEM Alerts query and subscription stream, asset management and query, digital apps for mobile and desktop notification, travel risk intelligence, safety devices, risk events, and the SnapComms internal-communications API.

**Areas:** Critical Event Management · Emergency Management · Mass Notification · Incident Management · IT Alerting · Public Warning · Risk Intelligence · Business Continuity · Employee Safety · Travel Risk Management

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Everbridge: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
